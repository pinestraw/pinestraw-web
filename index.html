How we work

Pine straw is what a forest drops to protect its own ground. We like that idea for software: useful, unshowy, and good for whatever grows under it.

  • Calm by default. No dark patterns, no noise for its own sake. Apps should feel lighter after you use them.
  • Built to last. Native where it matters, boring infrastructure where it doesn't, and code we can maintain for years.
  • Small teams. The people who design a product are the people who build and support it.
  • Your data is yours. We collect what a feature needs and nothing else.
