Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Pinestraw Enterprises LLC ("Pinestraw", "we", "us") handles information when you visit pinestraw.enterprises, email us, or text us. We collect what a feature needs and nothing else.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, email address, and mobile number, when you email us, text us, or fill out our contact form.
  • The content of your messages, including emails and text messages you send us and our replies.
  • SMS consent records: when and how you agreed to receive texts, and any opt-out (STOP) or opt-in (START) requests.
  • Basic technical data. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when you visit. We do not use advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send account and service notifications you asked for.
  • To honor opt-out requests and keep records of consent.
  • To keep our services secure and meet legal obligations.

Mobile numbers and SMS consent

We never share or sell your mobile number or SMS consent to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except the service providers that deliver our messages (such as our messaging provider and mobile carriers), and only so they can deliver them. See our SMS Terms for what we send and how to opt out.

When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers who host our systems or deliver email and text messages for us, under obligations to protect it;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and Pinestraw.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and contact details only as long as we need them to support you, and keep consent and opt-out records as long as needed to show we honored them.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages.
  • Email us to ask what information we hold about you, or to correct or delete it.
